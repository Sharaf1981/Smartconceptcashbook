Privacy Policy – Smart Concept Cashbook

Last updated: 29 September 2026

This policy explains how the Smart Concept Cashbook app (Android app and web version, "the app"), published by Smart Concept Architectural & Interior Design ("we"), handles information, including data received from Google APIs.

1. Information you enter in the app

Cash books, transactions, contacts, invoice photos, company details, reports and local user accounts are stored only on your device (in the app's private storage, or in your browser for the web version). We do not run servers that receive this data, and we do not collect analytics, advertising identifiers or location. Local account passwords are stored as salted hashes, never in plain text.

2. Google user data

If you choose "Sign in with Google" for backups, the app requests one permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file (see, edit, create and delete only the Google Drive files that you use with this app). The app uses it to:

  • create a folder named "Smart Concept Backups" in your Google Drive;
  • upload backup files of your app data into that folder, manually or on the schedule you choose;
  • list and download those backup files when you choose to restore them;
  • read your Google account e-mail address from the Drive API, only to display which account is linked.

The app cannot access any other files in your Drive. Sign-in tokens are stored only on your device and are sent only to Google.

3. How Google data is used, shared and stored

  • Google user data is used only to provide the backup and restore feature you requested.
  • It is transferred only between your device and Google. We do not receive it, sell it, share it with third parties, use it for advertising, or use it to develop, improve or train generalized or non-personalized AI or machine-learning models.
  • No person at Smart Concept reads your Google data.

Smart Concept Cashbook's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Your choices and deleting data

  • Stop backups to Google Drive at any time with "Sign out of Google" in the app's Backup screen; this also revokes the app's access.
  • You can also remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete backups by deleting the "Smart Concept Backups" folder or its files in Google Drive.
  • Uninstalling the app (or clearing the browser's site data for the web version) deletes all data stored on the device.

5. Security

All communication with Google uses HTTPS. The Android app talks only to Google's sign-in and Drive servers.

6. Children

The app is a business tool and is not directed to children under 13.

7. Changes

We will update this page if our practices change, and the date above will change accordingly.

٢. بيانات Google

عند اختيار «تسجيل الدخول بحساب Google» للنسخ الاحتياطي يطلب التطبيق صلاحية واحدة: الوصول إلى الملفات التي ينشئها التطبيق في Google Drive فقط. يستخدمها لإنشاء مجلد «Smart Concept Backups» ورفع ملفات النسخ الاحتياطي إليه، وعرضها وتنزيلها عند الاستعادة، وقراءة البريد الإلكتروني للحساب من Drive لعرض الحساب المرتبط فقط. لا يستطيع التطبيق رؤية أي ملف آخر في Drive.

٣. الاستخدام والمشاركة

تُستخدم بيانات Google فقط لتقديم خاصية النسخ والاستعادة، وتنتقل بين جهازك و Google فقط. لا نستلمها ولا نبيعها ولا نشاركها مع أي جهة، ولا نستخدمها للإعلانات أو لتدريب نماذج الذكاء الاصطناعي. يلتزم التطبيق بسياسة بيانات مستخدمي خدمات Google API بما فيها متطلبات الاستخدام المحدود (Limited Use).

٤. التحكم والحذف

يمكنك إيقاف الربط في أي وقت من «تسجيل الخروج من Google» في شاشة النسخ الاحتياطي، أو من myaccount.google.com/permissions. احذف مجلد «Smart Concept Backups» من Drive لحذف النسخ. حذف التطبيق يحذف كل بياناته من الجهاز.
