Smart Concept Cashbook
دفتر سمارت كونسبت

A cash-book and project accounting app made by Smart Concept Architectural & Interior Design for its own projects, staff and clients.

What the app does

  • Keeps a cash book for every project and a main cash safe: money in, money out and running balances.
  • Tracks suppliers and customers, with each contact's account statement across projects.
  • Records payment vouchers, receipts with photos of invoices, categories and payment methods.
  • Produces PDF and Excel reports with the company's letterhead.
  • Supports several companies, local user accounts for staff, and import from Excel.
  • Backs up the app's data to the user's own Google Drive.

How the app uses Google data

Signing in with Google is optional and is used only for backups. The app requests a single permission, Google Drive – files created by this app (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file). With it the app:

  • creates a folder named Smart Concept Backups in the user's Drive and uploads backup files (.backup) of the app's data into it;
  • lists and downloads those same backup files when the user chooses to restore;
  • reads the signed-in account's e-mail address from Drive only to show which account is linked.

The app cannot see or change any other file in the user's Drive. Google data is never sent to Smart Concept or to anyone else. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
